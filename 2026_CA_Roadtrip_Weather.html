⚠️ Key Weather Watch Items
🌡️ Redding Heat — Day 7
Redding regularly hits 95-100°F+ in June. Stay in the van's AC when possible, drink water constantly, and don't linger outside. The cool Eureka coast earlier that morning will feel like a different planet.
🔦 Subway Cave: 46°F Inside — Day 8
Regardless of the outside temperature, Subway Cave stays 46°F year-round. Bring a flashlight and jacket for every person. You'll go from a warm 80°F outside to pitch black and freezing inside.
🌫️ June Gloom — Days 2 & 5
Coastal fog is normal and expected in Monterey and SF. It typically burns off by noon. Morning Alcatraz ferry will be cold and windy on the water — jackets for all 7. Fog doesn't dampen the experience, it's part of the SF atmosphere.
☀️ Yosemite Heat — Day 10
Summer 2026 forecast is hotter and drier than normal with peak heat in late June. Start Mist Trail early, carry 2L+ of water per person. The Vernal Fall soaking is well earned and will feel amazing.
🏔️ Mammoth Altitude — Day 11
At 8,000ft, Mammoth sees 20-30°F temperature drops after sunset. Warm afternoon at Devils Postpile, cool birthday dinner. Watch younger kids for altitude headaches — extra water and take it slow at first.
🧊 Lassen Elevation — Day 8
Lassen NP overnight lows hit the mid-30s to 40°F in June even when daytime is pleasant. You're only visiting during the day so this mostly affects what to wear — layers you can shed as it warms up.

☀️ Overall Outlook
Outstanding weather window for the entire trip. Zero rain days expected across all 12 stops. California's June is historically its driest month, and 2026 is shaping up to be warmer and drier than normal — great news for your outdoor-heavy itinerary.
🌡️ Temperature Range
You'll experience everything from a brisk 40°F overnight at Lassen to 95°F in Redding — all in the same week. The key is layering. Cool coastal mornings give way to warm afternoons. High elevation sites (Lassen, Tuolumne, Mammoth) need jackets even in June.
🎯 Best Weather Days
Days 1, 9, and 12 are perfect weather days — warm, sunny, and exactly what you'd hope for. Day 10 in Yosemite will be hot but manageable if you start early. The coastal fog on Days 2 and 5 is atmospheric, not a problem.
